MEMBERSHIP RULES

How SPS Membership Works

No fine print. Just straight rules.

01
The Foundry Special — $2
Pay $2 today to reserve your spot and lock in the Foundry rate: 40% off your chosen tier for life. This offer is limited to founding spots only — once they're gone, full price applies to all new members.
02
30-Day Upgrade Window
From the day you're approved, you have 30 days to commit to your next tier and freeze that 40% rate. Simply click "Lock My [Tier] Rate" in your member dashboard. No payment required — just your commitment. Miss the window and standard pricing applies.
03
No Downgrades
Once you're on a tier, you can upgrade or cancel — but not downgrade. Each tier runs real infrastructure (your A.I.R.™ instance, dedicated agents, content access) that we can't partially unwind. If a tier no longer fits, we'll help you transition out gracefully.
04
Cancel = Lose Your Rate
Your Foundry rate is tied to your continuous membership. Cancel and it's gone — permanently. You can rejoin, but only at the current market rate. We offer a limited pause option so you never have to make that call in a rough month.
05
Membership Pause
Need a breather? You may pause your membership for up to 60 days per calendar year. Your A.I.R.™ goes into standby, your rate stays locked, and your spot is held. Pause is available once per 12-month period. See FAQ for full details.
06
Application & Approval
SPS is application-based. We review every submission to ensure the right fit between your goals and our capabilities. The $2 is processed at application. Approval typically takes 1–3 business days. If we're not the right match, we'll refund your $2, no questions asked.
07
Tier Availability
Each tier has a fixed number of Foundry spots. Spark: 500. Builder: 200. Venture: 100. Superpower: 50. Once a tier fills, new applicants join a waitlist at full market rate. Founding members always have priority for upgrades within the Foundry window.
08
Content & Events Access
All paid tiers include access to the SPS podcast library and select live events. Superpower members receive full All-Access — every event, every replay, every year, including the Annual Flagship. Access scales with your tier and persists as long as your membership is active.
